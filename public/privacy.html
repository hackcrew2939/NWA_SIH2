Privacy Policy

National Weather Analytics (NWA) | Official Domain: weatheranalytics.in | Effective: September 2026

1. Scope & Mandate

This Privacy Policy describes how National Weather Analytics ("NWA", "we", "our") collects, processes, and protects information submitted by users and gathered through open meteorological feeds across India. We operate on the principle of minimal data collection necessary to provide accurate localized atmospheric reporting on our official domain weatheranalytics.in.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the following limited categories of information:

  • Geolocation Data: When you select "Detect Current GPS" in the interface, your device latitude and longitude are used locally to query nearby meteorological stations. We do not store or track continuous device location logs.
  • Citizen Weather Submissions: Information you voluntarily provide when submitting a field report, including event category (heavy rain, flood, cyclone, heatwave), location landmark, incident description, reporter attribution name, and optional attached media.
  • Public Social Feeds: Public atmospheric advisories and severe weather notices tagged with official hashtags such as #IMD. Handles and personal accounts are anonymized prior to display or analytics ingestion.

3. How Data Is Used

Data gathered through this system is strictly utilized for public meteorological observation and disaster awareness:

  • Rendering localized weather forecasts and interactive station maps.
  • Validating citizen reports through editorial moderation queues to prevent false alerts.
  • Generating open tabular and document summaries for disaster response and civil preparedness.
  • We do not sell, license, or monetize any user data or reports to commercial advertising brokers.

4. Data Security & Retention

Field submissions and meteorological cache data are stored on secured infrastructure with restricted access controls. Field reports are retained solely for seasonal meteorological trend verification and public safety archives.
